GOOD TO KNOW

Common partnership questions

Do sponsorship packages have fixed prices?

No. Each event has different needs and capacity. We’ll discuss your goals and contribution, then confirm the scope and any investment in a written proposal.

Can we contribute products, services, or supplies?

In-kind contributions may be a good fit. They must be relevant to the event, approved in advance, and delivered according to the agreed timeline.

Can we collect attendee information or promote offers?

Any data collection, sampling, or promotional activity must be reviewed and explicitly approved before the event. Student privacy and campus policies come first.

How early should we get in touch?

As early as possible. Lead time depends on event date, production needs, and review approvals. The planning team will confirm deadlines for each opportunity.
